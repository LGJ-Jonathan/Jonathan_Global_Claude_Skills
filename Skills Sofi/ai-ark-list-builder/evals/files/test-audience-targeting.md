**WHO IS DEREK'S IDEAL CLIENT?**

Derek arranges SBA-backed financing in the $3M to $25M range. His target is established health, fitness, and wellness business owners who need capital for expansion, equipment, acquisitions, or working capital.

Must be true:
- Owns a gym, med spa, or health/wellness business
- Business has been operating for 3+ years (SBA requires 3 years of tax returns)
- Revenue of $500K+ annually
- Is the decision maker (owner, founder, CEO, president)
- Based in the United States

Instant disqualifiers:
- Located in Nevada or Arizona
- Business under 3 years old
- Revenue under $500K
- Employees at large corporate chains (not the owner)
- Solo practitioners with no employees
- Personal trainers, aestheticians, or therapists who work at someone else's business
